Design mocks · v0.3 · supersedes the “generic” v0.2 look

PixelPare —
v0.3 playful mocks

Pocket Workshop is the primary direction: a tiny tactile machine on a warm workbench where reviewing a memory is something you do with your hands, not a bin you empty. Chunky modules, a backlit light table, a three-detent video press and a printed receipt replace the sidebar, storage donut and green check. Liquid Glass appears only on floating toolbars; photos always sit on opaque white. Two alternates (Pip, The Memory Edit) are included so you can compare vibes.

Safety is unchanged and visible in every screen: nothing is removed without your approval; swipes and shortcuts only mark; favorites are protected; at least one keeper per group; sizes are estimates until a copy exists; originals stay until you review them; Calm mode keeps every feature and disclosure.

Mac — Video press

Three detents, a second reel
Signature interaction: the knob clicks into one of three detents with a single tick (optional wooden sound, off by default) and the plain-language settings update before any preview work begins. Pressing creates a second reel beside the original; originals are only offered for review after the new copy is verified. Estimates become Preview ready (sample-based) and finally Final.

04

Mac — The receipt

An itemised ledger, not a victory lap
Signature interaction: the receipt prints out of the slot once, line by line, and is then static. It lists kept and failed items as plainly as removed ones — “Originals kept” is a good outcome. Keeping both copies is a valid result and is never counted as space saved. Unknowns are printed as unknowns.

06

Mac — Calm mode

Same layout, same safety, quieter
Proof it exists: the light table above, with Calm on — flat neutral surfaces, no grain, no tab tilt or snap, no sounds or haptics, no easter eggs. Identical controls, identical disclosures. Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast and Dynamic Type are honoured independently of this toggle.
What changes
✓ Flat, neutral surfaces
✓ Stationary cards, no animation
✓ No sounds, no haptics
✓ Plain copy, no quips
✓ Same keeper, marks, protection & plan

What doesn’t
Every control, every safety disclosure, every estimate label.
