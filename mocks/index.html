PhotoSlim — UX mocks v0.2 (after Sol review)

Principle: one big number, one big button. Everything else is a card you can open, review, and undo. Mac first; iPhone uses the same flow and the same shared components.

1 · Mac — Home (“Free up space”)

First launch asks for Photos access, then scans thumbnails (fast; originals are not downloaded). Savings across cards are de-duplicated — each photo is counted once. The ring shows library composition; the hero number is the realistic, reviewable saving. Each card opens a focused review.

2 · Mac — Similar photos review

Each row is one group. Green KEEP = auto-picked best shot (sharpness, eyes open, aesthetics). Others are suggested; nothing counts until you approve the group (⏎). Double-click a group for a large side-by-side with linked zoom. Keyboard: ↑/↓ group, ←/→ photo, Space toggle. Thresholds are conservative — no strictness knob.

3 · Mac — Compress videos

Three factual presets — no quality promises. Savings show as Estimate → Preview ready → Final size; previews encode a few clips on demand. Already-efficient and unsupported (HDR, slo-mo) videos are skipped with reasons. No custom sliders in v1.

5 · Mac — Working & Done

Runs in the background (menu-bar progress). Queue resumes after reopening (the in-flight video restarts). Done screen is honest: no fake Undo, no purge nudge, and the over-quota caveat from Apple’s docs.

6 · iPhone — same flow, thumb-friendly

Home = same cards in a list. Similar photos become a swipe stack (← mark for removal, → keep, ↑ keep whole group) — swipes only mark; nothing is deleted until the final confirm, and buttons exist for every gesture with the group strip below. Compression presets live in a bottom sheet. Long jobs use iOS 26 continued background processing with Live Activity progress.
